Com2000 + BMining

AI Cloud Computing Mining Platform

Com2000, in partnership with BMining, plans to operate a cloud computing mining platform that allows investors to participate in Bitcoin mining without owning physical hardware.

Core Business Model

Fractional Computing Power — retail users purchase shares of hashrate for a fixed term.
Infrastructure Management — BMining handles hardware, electricity and maintenance.
Revenue Sharing — users receive mining profits after electricity and service fees.
Transparency — mining reports to track earnings.

Profitability Factors

Bitcoin Price — higher market values increase dollar value of rewards.
Network Difficulty — rising total hashrate reduces individual returns.
Operational Costs — energy efficiency and hardware maintenance impact net profit.
USA Legislative Impacts — "Mined in America" bills and 100% bonus depreciation favor U.S.-based certified miners.

The "Mined in America" Act (2026)

Introduced March 2026 — frames Bitcoin mining as critical U.S. industrial policy: voluntary certification under Dept. of Commerce, hardware decoupling from foreign adversaries, onshore manufacturing via NIST, and Strategic Bitcoin Reserve priority for certified domestic facilities.

Impact on International Cloud Mining

U.S. tariffs on foreign hardware reaching 24-36% in late 2025 raise capital costs for foreign hosting centers. Institutional investors increasingly seek "certified" U.S. hashpower for ESG and national-security compliance — a flight to compliance away from international pools.
